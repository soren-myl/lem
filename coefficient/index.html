How this is built

Association coefficient points come from two separate sources, and the second one is easy to miss. First, per-match points: in the play-off round — still a qualifying stage — a win is worth 1.0 point and a draw 0.5, exactly half of the 2/1/0 clubs earn once the league phase starts. Those count for both sides of a two-legged tie, added to whichever country regardless of aggregate result.

Second, and this is the correction from the previous version of this page: league-phase bonus points, awarded under UEFA's Annex D.5 by final ranking in the 36-team table (1st through 36th) once the league phase ends in January. Champions League ranges from 12.000 points down to a guaranteed floor of 6.000 even for a last-place finish — so the instant a club secures a CL place, its country has already banked at least 6.000 points, win or lose from here. Europa League and Conference League have no such floor: their bottom band is 0.000, so qualifying there pays off only through the matches actually played, exactly as this week's swing figures already assume.

country's season points = Σ (match points + secured league-phase bonus, per club) ÷ (clubs that country entered this season)

That divisor is fixed for the season once qualifying paths are set. This week's "range" per country is simply what's still live: each remaining alive club's leg-two result (0, 0.5 or 1.0), summed and divided by the club count — the Champions League bonus is already reflected in the standing totals below for every country that has clinched a CL place. The much bigger prize a qualification unlocks is the eight further matches at full value plus that January ranking bonus — the real lever for next year's ranking, and one this page can't finalise until the league phase actually plays out.
